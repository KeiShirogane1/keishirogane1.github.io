What is inside QwerNFC Restore

Scan RESTORE QRLoads the correct store and permanent recovery identity.
Restore QRCreates the exact same permanent QR replacement. It never changes the permanent qrValue or encodes the final destination directly.
Restore NFCRewrites the store's permanent managed NFC identity to a compatible NFC tag.

Instructions

  1. Download and install QwerNFC Restore.
  2. Open the app and tap Scan RESTORE QR.
  3. Scan this same store-specific RESTORE QR.
  4. Confirm the store name and QR ID shown in the app.
  5. Choose Restore QR or Restore NFC.
The private GitHub database and credentials are never placed in the customer APK or this public page.
