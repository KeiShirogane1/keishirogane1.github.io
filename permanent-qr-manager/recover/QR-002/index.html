How recovery works

  1. Install or open QwerNFC Restore.
  2. Authenticate as the owner.
  3. The app loads this store and its private backup from private GitHub storage.
  4. Restore QR verifies the physical permanent QR with the camera; the QR pattern stays exactly the same.
  5. Restore NFC restores the destination and then asks you to hold the NFC tag to the phone.
Do not share your owner PIN or GitHub authorization. This public page intentionally does not expose the private backup destination.
